# L3 — Skeleton and pelt

Source: L0 FS-01. Counts: 7 [CANON] / 6 [INFERRED].

P1 [INFERRED] -> skeleton has a 20-tick one-metre bar at left; pelt has the same bar at lower left. Both exceed 20 m.
P2 [CANON] -> the skull-and-spine frame and the funerary hide read as a death symbol; no mausoleum is included.
F1 [CANON] -> hidden in both assets. The flame organ is in L2; the pale breath is in L0.
F2 [CANON] -> the pelt keeps all eight glyph sites: crown, throat, left wing root, right wing root, mid-dorsal, left flank, right flank and tail. Gold glyphs are on scale fields, umber glyphs on white feather fields.
F3 [CANON] -> the omen is in the ash-grey seams and funerary sigils on the pelt; the mausoleum is in L0.
F4 [CANON] -> the skeleton has no surface; the pelt shows dark green-black scale fields and continuous white feather fields.
Q1 [CANON] -> exactly 2 wings: two wing girdles and two wing skeletons; exactly 2 wing sleeves on the pelt.
Q2 [INFERRED] -> exactly 1 head: one skull and exactly 1 head opening on the pelt.
Q3 [CANON] -> exactly 2 eyes: two empty orbits in the skull. The pelt has no eye holes; the eyes are removed with the head.
Q4 [INFERRED] -> exactly 1 tail: one vertebral tail chain and exactly 1 tail opening on the pelt.
Q5 [INFERRED] -> exactly 0 legs: no limb bones, no pelvis, no leg holes.
Q6 [INFERRED] -> exactly 0 horns: no horn cores, no horn holes.
N1 [INFERRED] -> both girdles sit behind the skull at the shoulders; both sleeves open from the shoulder region, not the head.

SKELETON: one serpentine spine, one skull with two orbits, two wing girdles marked by pale mint dashed ellipses with diamond tabs, two wing skeletons, one tail chain; no legs and no horns. A faint dashed body envelope shows that the short curved ribs stay inside the trunk and are not legs.

PELT: one continuous sheet; 1 head opening; 2 wing sleeves; 1 tail opening; 0 leg holes; 0 horn holes. Dark green-black scale fields and white feather fields continue across every dashed seam; the symbols stay attached to the same scale and feather regions. Each sleeve ends in a cuff opening; those two cuffs are wing sleeves, not extra heads or tails.
