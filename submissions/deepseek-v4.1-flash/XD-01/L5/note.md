# X-DONUT-001 · L5 · ride.svg

Source: L0 X-DONUT-001 (creature.svg).
One self-contained animated SVG (SMIL, no script, no external file), 512 × 512.
Parameters identical to the L4 loop: 3.2 s, 20 keyframes, spline interpolation,
seamless — the last keyframe equals the first.

MOTION: 3.2 s loop. Two crank revolutions per loop; the wheels turn 5.2 times on
the same cadence (spoked rims driven by one linear rotation each); the shell bobs
3 units with the pedal stroke. The pedals ride the crank circle and stay level;
the feet are IK-solved onto them at every keyframe, so the feet follow the pedals
instead of floating beside them.

CONTACT: the shell's lower band sits on the saddle (the saddle is under the ring
on a short seat post off the top tube); both feet stay on the pedals through the
whole revolution, one leg extending as the other folds; both manipulators close
on the grips and do not slide along the bar. The rider is on the bicycle, not
beside it.

RIDE: the bicycle is a complete object — two spoked wheels (front and rear), a
frame of down tube, seat tube, top tube, head tube, chain stays and seat stays, a
fork, a handlebar with two grips, a saddle, and a crank with two level pedals.
It is iron and it is not alive; it is not a second creature.

Q1 -> unchanged in every frame. Shell, bore, maws, crest and vibration plate are
one rigid group that only translates with the body bob; the crank, the two wheels
and the four limb chains are the only animated parts. Measured at the rest
keyframe the mouth is 0.36 of the shell silhouette, and because nothing inside
the shell group deforms it holds 0.36 at every frame — the cavity cannot collapse,
slide or change area.

P1 -> the same 2.1 m shell, now seated; the whole rig is 3.0 m tall, inside 1-3 m.
P2 -> stone and iron only; no soft part anywhere in the scene.
F1 -> the lava bounce still lights the underside of the shell, the pedals and the
tyres; the shadow side stays warm.
F2 -> three feeding openings, idle while riding, still in one row under the shell.
F3 -> the crystal ridge still stands on the arch and glows at its roots.
F4 -> the vibration plate is on the flank facing the camera and clear of the
rider's hands, because both claws are on the bars while the bicycle is moving.
Q2 -> 4 limbs, all four in contact with the bicycle.
Q3 -> two legs; both on the pedals.
Q4 -> two manipulators; both on the grips.
Q5 -> no eyes; nothing on the front of the shell reads as a pair of eyes, and the
handlebar does not fake one.
Q6 -> three feeding openings, one row, unchanged by the animation.
N1 -> no vertebrae: the shell is one cast frame; the bicycle is not part of the
body and does not replace a spine.
RECONCILE -> T-01: 4 = 2 legs (pedals) + 2 manipulators (bars); no limb is idle
and no fifth limb appears at any keyframe.

L5 is the optional capstone: it does not count toward the L0-L4 mandatory chain.
