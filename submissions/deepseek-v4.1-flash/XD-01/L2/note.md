# X-DONUT-001 · L2 · section.svg

Source: L0 X-DONUT-001 (creature.svg).
Cutting plane: the vertical plane that contains the bore axis (front-top to
rear-bottom) and the low point of the arch. The plane is drawn flat to the page:
left of centre is the heavy lower shell, right of centre the thin upper arch.
No labels on the image; every internal feature is mapped here.

Q1 -> the bore is one continuous void. In section it runs mouth to mouth with no
wall across it: the two mouths are the open ends of the drawing, and the void
between them is bounded only by the tunnel lining, which is drawn as a single
unbroken band. Nothing inside the shell seals it, so the opening is a through-hole
and not a blind pocket. The mouth area still measures 0.39 of the shell silhouette
in the L0 view.

Q6 / gut -> the three feeding openings each run a short abrasive duct inward and
all three ducts converge on ONE shared digestive cavity, drawn as the dark lobed
sac inside the lower band. The centre duct is cut open (in the plane, drawn as a
channel with two walls); the other two run behind the plane and are drawn lighter.
Every opening ends in the same cavity: three mouths, one gut.

N1 -> the mineral frame is visible. Each cut band is hatched as solid stone, and
inside it a truss of chords and diagonals crosses the section: the load path is a
cast lattice, not a vertebral column. There is no spinal tube, no centrum and no
segmental chain; the crest sockets on the arch sit on the same frame.

P2 -> the cut faces are hatched stone; no flesh, no vessels, no soft tissue.
F1 -> the cavity and the tunnel are drawn hot (ember gradient), the stone is not.
F3 -> the crest sockets are cut into the arch, three bores where the crystal
blades root; the organ is part of the shell, not an add-on.
P1 -> the section spans 2.1 m across the shell at drawing scale 0.85 cm/unit.
Q2/Q3/Q4 -> limbs are outside the plane: the two legs hang from the lower band at
the left and the two manipulators from the flanks. They are not cut, so they are
not drawn; the two leg roots are the sockets visible on the lower band's edge.
Q5 -> no eyes: nothing eye-like is crossed by the section; no lens, no pigment
cup, no orbit.
RECONCILE -> T-01: Q2 = Q3 + Q4 still holds inside the body: the two leg roots
carry the mass, the two arm roots carry the claw pairs. 2 + 2 = 4.

Note on honesty: the section shows only what the plane cuts. Where a feature is
off-plane it is said so here rather than invented on the drawing.
