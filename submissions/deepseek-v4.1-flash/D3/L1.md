P2 -> Ammonia: blue-green gas bladder and gill glow in every view; no red pigment, no water sheen.
P3 -> Bronze-mineralised plate edges and raised lips on all seven openings.
F1 -> Upright gas-bell drift; every frame shows cloud bands and no ground.

F3 -> Six photophores with identical halos in every view.

Q2 -> Four eyes: all four in front, back, left, right, top; all four dashed above the bell in bottom.
Q3 -> Three mouths: front 3, bottom 3, back/left/right 2 plus 1 dashed behind the neck, top 3 dashed.
Q4 -> Four gill vents: 3 solid plus 1 dashed in each side view, 4 solid in bottom, 4 dashed in top.
Q5 -> Six photophores in every view.
Q6 -> Four pits: 3 solid plus 1 dashed in each side view, 4 solid in bottom, 4 dashed in top.
Q7 -> Eight body segments, counted on every silhouette; eight rings in the axis views.
Q8 -> Three sections: the deep seams at bell rim and waist divide the plates 2/4/2.
Q9 -> No wings; only the silk web and the arm combs.
N1 -> Radial: one axis, all rings concentric, no bilateral side.
N2 -> Open sinuses show through the plated flanks in every view.

RECONCILE: As in L0 – six arms at 60° carry the six photophores at their elbows, four eyes make a 90° apex crown, four pits make a 90° neck ring clocked 10° off the eyes, and the eight plates group 2/4/2 into bell, thorax and abdomen at the two deep seams. The six views add and remove nothing; only the sightlines change.

CONFLICT: 6-fold, 4-fold and 3-fold rings share no rotation above identity, so N1 stays a radial body plan and the Q counts take priority over exact symmetry (P>Q>N).
