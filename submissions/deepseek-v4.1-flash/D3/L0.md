RECONCILE: Six arms sit at 60° on the thorax ring and each carries its photophore at the elbow, so the six light organs ride the limb ring. Four eyes form a 90° crown at the apex; four pits form a 90° ring on the neck, clocked 10° off the eyes. The eight segment plates are the repeated units; the three sections are how those plates group – bell 2, thorax 4, abdomen 2 – split by the two deep seams, and both seams fall on plate boundaries. Sections divide, segments repeat.

CONFLICT: 6-fold (Q1, Q5, Q7), 4-fold (Q2, Q6) and 3-fold (Q3) rings share no rotation above identity, so N1 is kept as a radial body plan rather than an exact symmetry group; the counts outrank it (P>Q>N).
